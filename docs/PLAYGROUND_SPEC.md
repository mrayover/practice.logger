# Playground specification

The current Log destination offers Journal and Playground. Playground opens a Coming Soon screen and returns to Journal. The collectible workflow below requires a separately assigned increment.

- Each completed practice Block placement earns one collectible Block. Its historical record is independent of the reusable Block definition. Preserve originating Block and session identity, completion time, and the Goal and priority at completion.
- Before completion, the potential reward color follows the current Goal priority. Freeze the earned color at completion and retain the historical entry's frozen right-side color slit.
- G1 first-accrual Goal attribution records a separate practice-time fact. P2 completion snapshots record the Goal, priority and color at completion. A future migration must use the completion facts for reward attribution and define eligibility for older records before issuing collectibles. Do not substitute a current Goal or a first-accrual snapshot for missing completion facts.
- Make the Playground flat, zoomable and pannable. Collectible Blocks move freely. Selecting one opens its recorded information in a bottom panel.
- Expand the available space at milestones. Proposed thresholds are 1, 10, 20, 40, 80, 100, and later milestones.
- Positions are mutable. Earned history is permanent. The future migration must preserve stable reward identity and prevent duplicate issuance across retries or reopen. Complete ZIP backup must preserve the collection and its layout.
